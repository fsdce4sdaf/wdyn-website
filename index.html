How it works

Search becomes a conversation.

Instead of forcing people to understand a store’s taxonomy, the app understands the request and gathers the missing details one step at a time.

01

Say what you need

Start naturally: “glasses”, “a mountain bike”, or a more detailed request.

02

Refine naturally

The assistant asks only for useful constraints such as audience, budget, brand or other category-specific criteria.

03

Understand intent

Your answers are translated into structured product-search criteria behind the scenes.

04

See matched products

Relevant options are surfaced directly in the conversation, with images, names and prices.
